## Reply text

Sent 2026-11-09 with the resubmission of 0.0.1 (6).

Hello, and thank you for reviewing KnowMe.

1. Recording: https://youtu.be/NPIfyLfMYqE (unlisted, 3 minutes). It shows KnowMe 0.0.1 (build 3) on iPhone with iOS 27.2, captured in the iOS Simulator. It starts at launch and shows onboarding, setup, logging a moment, "I'm in it", Patterns, Reflect, the crisis support card, and the privacy/terms links. The app has no accounts, no login, no shared user content and no in-app purchases. This resubmission is build 6. It works the same as the recording, with one small addition: Me → Get support now has its own tappable "Text 988" and "Text 1-800-MY-RESET" links next to the call links.

2. Purpose and audience: KnowMe is a private journal for adults who want to understand a habit they are changing (for example cannabis, nicotine, alcohol, or one they name). In about ten seconds they log a moment: how strong the urge was, where they were, how they felt, whether they did it, and what helped. The app shows their own patterns back in plain language. The problem it solves is that habit apps tend to shame slips or give generic advice; KnowMe is a calm mirror that keeps the data on the phone. It gives no medical advice and makes no treatment claims.

3. Setup and access: no account or credentials are needed. Open the app, step through the short intro, pick a habit and optionally add a reason. To see every feature quickly, open Me → "Load demo data", then visit Patterns and tap "Reflect on my week". Reflect writes one letter per day; a second tap the same day shows a "try again tomorrow" note by design. Typing a crisis phrase (for example "I want to die") in any note field shows a 988/911 support card before anything is saved.

4. External services: one, used only when the person taps Reflect. The app sends an aggregate weekly summary (counts, hours, top tags; no raw notes unless they opt in for that letter) to our own proxy on Vercel (knowme-proxy.vercel.app), which calls Meta's Llama 3.3 70B model hosted by DeepInfra and returns a short reflection. Everything else, including storage (on-device SQLite), patterns and the crisis check, runs on the device. No analytics, ads, crash reporting, authentication or payment SDKs.

5. Regional differences: none. The app works the same in every region. The built-in support numbers (988, SAMHSA 1-800-662-4357, 1-800-QUIT-NOW) are US services. Me → Get support also offers "Tell someone you trust", which works anywhere.

6. Regulated industry: not applicable. KnowMe is a self-reflection journal, not a medical device or treatment, and was declared not a regulated medical device in App Information. It uses no protected third-party material.

Thank you,
Michael Morahan, Freio Labs
