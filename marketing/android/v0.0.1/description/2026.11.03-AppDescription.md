## Full description (4,000)
```
Know yourself. Change follows.

KnowMe is a calm, private journal for people who want to understand a habit — not be lectured about it.

Maybe it's evenings that keep catching you out. Maybe it's stress at work, boredom at home, or a parent trying to stay patient when frustration builds. KnowMe doesn't hand you a plan. It helps you notice your own pattern, in your own words, one honest moment at a time.

★ LOG A MOMENT IN TEN SECONDS
When an urge or a hard moment shows up, note how strong it is, where you are, how you feel, whether you rode it out, and what helped. One screen, one thumb, done — even mid-moment.

★ WHEN IT'S HARD, START HERE
Tap "I'm in it" and KnowMe shows you back your own reasons, what has helped you before, and a slow breathing circle with a quiet timer. Your words first. No orders, no lectures.

★ SEE YOUR PATTERNS IN PLAIN LANGUAGE
KnowMe turns your moments into simple, local charts: intensity over time, when moments tend to land, where they happen, and what actually helps. Underneath, one plain sentence says what the chart shows — like "Most of your moments land between 7 and 10pm."

★ A GENTLE WEEKLY REFLECTION
When you choose to tap Reflect, KnowMe builds a brief, anonymous summary of your week and a short reflection is written from it, ending with one open question. Your notes are never included unless you turn that on for that one reflection.

★ THE GNOME NEVER JUDGES
Gnomie holds the lantern, never the lecture. There are no streaks to break, no red failure screens, and nothing is ever called a relapse or a failure. You rode it out, or you used — both are simply noted, and you keep going.

★ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you log — habits, reasons, moments — is stored only on this phone. There is no account, no sign-up, no cloud sync, no analytics, and no advertising. There is nothing to sell and nowhere for your journal to leak to. The patterns in KnowMe work fully offline.

★ MAKE IT YOURS
Track what matters to you, in your own words. Start with a preset, or name your own behaviour, feeling, or helper. KnowMe fits around your life — parenting patience, evening habits, smoking, drinking, scrolling, or anything else you want to understand better.

★ ONE PRICE. YOURS TO KEEP.
KnowMe is $7.99, once. No subscription, no tiers, no upsells. Pay once through Google Play and it's yours.

Welcome to a fresh, honest look at yourself.

Know thyself. The data is yours. The conclusions are yours.

—
KnowMe is a self-reflection and habit-tracking journal. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and it is not a replacement for professional support. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or call 911 in an emergency. Support lines are always one tap away in the app, under Me → Get support.
```
