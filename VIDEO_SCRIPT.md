# KnowMe — 60-Second Video Script

Matches [DEMO.md](DEMO.md), compressed from 3 minutes to 60 seconds. Same beats, same lines, faster cuts.
~150 spoken words at a calm pace. Record the phone, not your face. Phone on Do Not Disturb.

**Before:** Me → turn on demo data. Proxy live, `EXPO_PUBLIC_PROXY_URL` set. Dark room if you can — the app is designed for night.

| Time | Do (on screen) | Say (voiceover, calm, unhurried) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Open the app on **Today**. Hold on the big days number. | "This is KnowMe. A private journal for changing a habit. Calm, almost empty — nothing asking for attention." |
| 0:08–0:20 | Tap **I'm in it**. Reasons fade in, breath circle starts. Stay on it. | "In a hard moment, it doesn't lecture you. It shows you your own reasons, what helped last time, and room to breathe." |
| 0:20–0:30 | Close it. Go to **Log**. Tap intensity, context, feelings, Save — under 10 seconds. | "Logging a craving takes ten seconds. One thumb. Even mid-craving." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Open **Patterns**. Pause on the by-hour chart and its plain sentence. | "Then it shows you your patterns, in plain language. Most of your cravings land between 7 and 10pm." |
| 0:40–0:52 | Open **Reflect**. Tap Reflect. Letter appears line by line. Hold on the final question. | "Once a week, Llama mirrors that back — from a private summary of counts and patterns. Never your raw notes. And it ends with one question, not advice." |
| 0:52–1:00 | Log a note with a crisis phrase. The **988 card** fills the screen. Cut to black on it, or back to Today. | "And if someone types that they're in crisis, safety comes before everything. Nothing is saved, nothing is sent. Know thyself." |

## Notes

- **Do not rush 0:52.** The crisis card is the trust beat. Let it sit for a full second before "Know thyself."
- No music with lyrics. If any: one soft piano or ambient bed, low. Silence is also fine — it matches the app.
- No feature tour, no settings screens, no code. If a beat runs long, cut words from 0:30–0:40 first ("in plain language" line), never from the opening or the crisis beat.
- Captions on: several lines land better read than heard ("Nothing is saved, nothing is sent").
- End card (optional, 2s, not counted): `KnowMe — Know thyself.` on `#0F1115`.
